## Key Hands-On Findings

- Installation/onboarding was easy.
- PageFly presented a plan-choice screen before dashboard access.
- Free plan shown in-app: $0, 1 published slot, 10 AI credits, 320+ templates, 200+ integrations, 24/7 live chat.
- Builder: $24/month from 5 published slots, 80 AI credits, A/B testing, AI translation, CRO analytics; extra slots shown as $5 per additional 5.
- Accelerate: $99/month, unlimited pages/sections, 320 AI credits, plus heatmaps, AI page checkup, AEO optimizer and sales funnel.
- Enterprise: $299/month, 1,600 AI credits/month for one store plus priority/support features.
- Reviewer first attempted AI page generation but the Free plan's 10-credit allowance was insufficient for the attempted full-page workflow; reviewer switched to a template.
- Template used: “Juicy – New Year.”
- Editor was described by reviewer as very easy to use.
- Full page took roughly 30–40 minutes to build with planning done beforehand.
- Responsive preview was checked at mobile/tablet/desktop breakpoints.
- Important publishing finding: when the PageFly page was published as Home, existing theme-controlled header/hero content remained alongside PageFly content and the resulting homepage looked visually inconsistent. Do not claim no workaround exists; only report the observed default publishing result and that no workaround was verified in this test.
- Analytics displayed real dev-store data.
- AI page checkup returned a health score.
- Section performance showed real data.
- A/B testing was accessible/usable while reviewer was on Free, despite the verified pricing interface listing A/B testing under Builder. Report the discrepancy factually without speculating why.
- AEO optimizer interface stated it currently works on product pages.

## Editorial Judgment

### Strengths

1. Approachable editor for non-technical merchants.
2. Large template/element ecosystem.
3. Useful CRO/analytics tooling integrated into the page-building workflow.
4. Fast enough to build a substantial page in one session.

### Limitations

1. Free AI-credit allowance was restrictive in the tested AI-generation workflow.
2. Home-page publishing created a design-continuity issue with the existing Shopify theme in this test.
3. Feature gating was inconsistent between observed Free-plan access and the pricing interface for A/B testing.

### Best Fit

Non-technical Shopify merchants building individual landing, promotional, or campaign pages without a developer.

### Poor Fit

Merchants expecting a completely cohesive full-homepage redesign with no theme-level reconciliation or merchants requiring extensive AI generation on the Free plan.

### Alternatives Named, Not Tested

GemPages, EComposer, Shogun.
