This document records Blinto's **preliminary category-owner structure and validation shortlist** for the Shopify App Review program.

It follows SOP 1. A category or app appearing here is **not automatically an approved review topic**. Final topic approval still requires the SOP 1 checks for search opportunity, intent, merchant relevance, Blinto business relevance, testability/evidence, differentiation, founder relationship potential, resource feasibility, and content-cluster value.

## 6. What Must Happen Before These Become ClickUp Assignments

For each of the first five candidates, complete the remaining SOP 1 validation:

1. Confirm primary keyword and search intent.
2. Review current SERP competition and content freshness.
3. Confirm differentiation opportunity for Blinto.
4. Confirm current app availability and testability.
5. Confirm founder/company and partnership relevance where applicable.
6. Complete the full 10-factor / 50-point SOP 1 scorecard.
7. Approve or replace the candidate.
8. Only then create/assign the ClickUp review task and begin SOP 2 research/testing.

## 7. Important Rule

Do **not** treat the remaining 20 apps as an automatic production queue. They are the category shortlist. The first five reviews are the operational pilot. After those five move through the complete workflow, Blinto should inspect process friction and quality before assigning the next batch.
