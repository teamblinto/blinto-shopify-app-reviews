# Review Lifecycle & ClickUp Operating Model

This is the approved lightweight ClickUp lifecycle for the Blinto Shopify App Review pilot.

## Principle

**One owner → one review → one final SEO/quality check → publish.**

ClickUp should make work visible, not reproduce every SOP step as a board column.

## Production Board Scope

The **Shopify App Reviews** list contains actual review-production tasks only.

Planning, category selection, topic validation, distribution strategy, and program/system improvements belong in **Shopify GTM Execution**.

## Final Production Statuses

1. **Assigned**
2. **Research & Write**
3. **SEO Check**
4. **Ready to Publish**
5. **Published**
6. **Monitoring**

### Assigned

One primary author/assignee owns the review. App, category, author, due date, and working link should be known.

### Research & Write

The same author owns research/testing, evidence, brief as needed, writing, and self-check. These are working steps, not separate ClickUp statuses.

When the draft and author self-check are complete, the author moves the task to **SEO Check**.

### SEO Check

Shemanto, or later a calibrated SEO Agent, performs the single routine final review gate. It covers SEO readiness and obvious editorial/evidence/quality issues.

If changes are required, keep the task in **SEO Check**. The original author resolves comments and resubmits. Do not create a separate Revision status.

Material technical, legal, reputational, or specialist concerns can be escalated when necessary without adding a routine board stage for every article.

When passed, Shemanto/SEO Agent moves the task to **Ready to Publish**.

### Ready to Publish

Required pilot checks have passed. Fazle or Shemanto can complete preview/publishing QA and publish.

### Published

The article is publicly live and applicable live-page QA is complete.

### Monitoring

The published article is under performance/freshness ownership according to SOP 7.

## Who Changes the Status?

**The person handing the work to the next owner changes the status.**

| Transition | Changed by |
|---|---|
| Create/assign → Assigned | Fazle/Shemanto |
| Assigned → Research & Write | Author |
| Research & Write → SEO Check | Author |
| SEO Check → Ready to Publish | Shemanto/SEO Agent |
| Ready to Publish → Published | Fazle/Shemanto |
| Published → Monitoring | Publisher/maintenance owner |

## Corrections

Corrections do not need a board column. During SEO Check, feedback is recorded in the task/review record, the author fixes it, and the task stays in SEO Check until passed.

## Blockers

Use a blocker field/comment rather than adding blocker statuses. Useful blocker types include app access, paid feature/budget, evidence, specialist validation, SEO input, author correction, publishing asset, and external dependency.

## ClickUp Task Structure

Use **one ClickUp task per review**.

Recommended task name:

`[App Name] Shopify App Review`

Keep task information minimal:

- App/review title
- Primary author/assignee
- Category
- Due date
- Current status
- Blocker if any
- Review Hub/GitHub working link
- Private preview when available
- Published URL when live

Do not duplicate the article or full SOP checklists inside ClickUp.

## Dashboard

For the pilot, the board only needs to answer:

- Who owns each review?
- What is actively being researched/written?
- What is waiting for SEO check?
- What is ready to publish?
- What is published/being monitored?
- What is blocked or overdue?

Avoid a complex dashboard until the five-review pilot shows what is actually useful.

## Management Rule

ClickUp tracks execution; GitHub/Review Hub tracks the work and evidence. Status movement never substitutes for completing the underlying evidence and quality requirements.
