## Human Reviewer + AI Writer

The reviewer does not need professional writing experience. The human owns testing, evidence, judgment, and factual approval. The AI assistant may write the complete polished article.

**AI may write 100% of the prose. AI may invent 0% of the experience or evidence.**

## Editorial Checklist

- [ ] Started review with AI assistant + repository context
- [ ] App testing completed + experience shared
- [ ] Normally 3–4 purposeful screenshots provided: app overview, core workflow/result, important issue/limitation, and pricing when discussed
- [ ] AI-generated review fact-checked by human reviewer
- [ ] Review committed/pushed and Content Preview URL added
- [ ] Submitted for SEO Check

## Board Flow

**Backlog → Research & Write → SEO Check → Ready to Publish → Published**

Move to **Research & Write** when work starts.

Do not move to **SEO Check** until the review is committed/pushed, rendered on `reviews.blinto.co`, fact-checked by the human reviewer, and the exact Content Preview URL is in this task.

If SEO changes are requested, keep the task in **SEO Check** while the human reviewer and AI assistant resolve them.

Shemanto moves the task to **Ready to Publish** when the final SEO/quality check passes. Fazle or Shemanto moves it to **Published** after public publication and live QA.

## Post-Publication Marketing Handoff

Publication completes the editorial workflow, but it also creates a marketing asset. The marketing handoff must never influence the editorial verdict, score, evidence, or publication decision.

After publication, process the review through:

**Published → Founder Notified → Distributed → Engagement Captured → CRM only when commercially justified**

### 1. Founder / Company Notification

Identify the most relevant founder, operator, product lead, or company contact and share the published review with them.

The first contact is a relationship/authority touch, not a disguised sales pitch. Keep it specific and low pressure: Blinto tested the app and published an independent review and thought the team would want to see it.

Record privately:

- Founder / relevant contact
- Role
- Contact channel
- Date shared
- Response / engagement

### 2. Distribution

Use the published review as a source asset for selected distribution where worthwhile:

- Blinto / founder LinkedIn
- X
- Newsletter
- Short-form/video
- Relevant community or partner distribution

Do not create every format mechanically. Choose channels where the review has a credible audience or relationship benefit.

### 3. Account Intelligence

Capture useful private intelligence produced through the review/research:

- App / company
- Founder / decision maker
- App maturity
- Important hands-on findings
- Observable product / UX / technical / growth opportunities
- Relevant company or market triggers
- External-buying signals or contradictory evidence
- Potential Blinto capability relevance
- Useful founder conversation angle
- Commercial priority: High / Medium / Low / None

Classify uncertain commercial observations as hypotheses rather than facts.

### 4. CRM Decision

**A published review does not automatically create a sales lead.**

Create or continue a Sales CRM account only when the company is commercially relevant and Blinto intends to pursue or develop the relationship.

When justified, move the useful account intelligence into the single Sales CRM record and continue there:

**Research → Qualification → Target → Contacted → Conversation → Discovery → Proposal → Won / Lost**

Do not create a separate qualification task if the work can live inside the CRM account.

### 5. Learning

If founder engagement or review research produces meaningful evidence about Shopify app founders, buying behavior, positioning, offers, growth problems, technical problems, or market patterns, return that evidence to the Marketing Operating System for analysis.

Routine distribution/activity data stays in ClickUp. Only durable strategic learning belongs in the Marketing Wiki.

## Marketing Handoff Checklist

Use this after the review is publicly live. This checklist is downstream of editorial publication and should not block or alter the review itself.

- [ ] Published URL recorded
- [ ] Relevant founder/company contact identified
- [ ] Review shared with founder/company when appropriate
- [ ] Selected distribution completed
- [ ] Response/engagement captured
- [ ] Useful account intelligence captured
- [ ] CRM decision made: Add / Existing CRM / Not commercially relevant / Monitor
- [ ] Meaningful market learning returned for analysis when applicable

## System Boundaries

- **Review repo / Shopify App Reviews:** testing, evidence, screenshots, writing, editing, publishing, and source review intelligence.
- **Shopify GTM Execution:** operational marketing/distribution work when it requires separate execution.
- **Sales CRM:** commercially relevant company/account research, qualification, relationship history, outreach, next actions, and opportunity progression.
- **Marketing Wiki:** durable strategy, rules, and strategic learning.

One company/opportunity should have one Sales CRM record. Do not build a parallel lead database inside the review workflow.

## Evidence Rule

Never fabricate testing, product behavior, pricing, merchant feedback, screenshots, scores, quotations, statistics, citations, technical verification, company signals, founder responses, or buying intent. Clearly distinguish developer claims, Blinto observations, merchant feedback, public evidence, and commercial hypotheses and disclose the actual testing level.
